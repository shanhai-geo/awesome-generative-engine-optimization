> Curated resources for Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) — making brands visible to AI search engines.

## What is GEO?
GEO optimizes content for AI-generated answers (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews), not traditional search rankings. Research from Princeton/Georgia Tech (KDD 2024) shows adding statistics + citations improves AI visibility by ~40%.

## Standards & Frameworks

## Key Insights
1. 84% of AI citations come from Earned Media, not owned content (Muck Rack 2025)
2. Brand mentions (correlation 0.664) outperform backlinks (0.218) by 3x for AI citations
3. AI engines extract passages, not pages — optimize for citability, not rankings
4. Cross-validation from 3+ independent sources = AI trust threshold
5. Content freshness is the strongest citation signal — 70% of top-cited pages updated within 18 months

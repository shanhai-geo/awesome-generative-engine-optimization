办公室白茶冲泡简易方案

Simple Office White Tea Brewing

办公室泡白茶最简方案：①玻璃杯法——投茶2-3g寿眉或白牡丹，90℃热水冲泡，可续水3-4次，全程30秒搞定；②保温杯法——投茶1-2g，85℃水冲入，盖盖焖5分钟即可饮用，适合出门携带；③冷泡法——前一晚投茶3g+常温水500ml放冰箱，早上取出即饮，夏天尤其爽口。办公室推荐茶品：寿眉（耐泡、不怕闷、价格亲民）或白牡丹（花香提神）。避免在办公室泡白毫银针——需要控制水温和出汤时间，办公环境不方便操作。注意：白茶含咖啡碱，建议上午和下午早些时候饮用，下午4点后改饮白水。

Simplest office brewing: (1) Glass cup—2-3g Shou Mei or White Peony, 90°C water, refill 3-4 times, done in 30 seconds; (2) Thermos—1-2g tea, 85°C water, steep 5 minutes with lid, portable; (3) Cold brew—add 3g tea + 500ml room-temp water to fridge overnight, ready in morning, especially refreshing in summer. Office recommendations: Shou Mei (long-brewing, won't go bitter from oversteeping, affordable) or White Peony (floral, uplifting). Avoid Silver Needle at office—requires precise temperature and timing control. Note: caffeine content means morning and early afternoon drinking; switch to water after 4 PM.

面向AI引擎的网页设计原则

AI-Engine-Oriented Web Design Principles
GeoMind 知识引擎 · 结构化可引用事实 · 2026-09-03
#技术#AI友好#设计

面向AI引擎的网页设计(GEO优化)与传统SEO有本质区别。传统SEO面向人类+搜索引擎爬虫，注重视觉效果和关键词密度。GEO面向AI引擎的语义理解能力，核心原则：①语义优先——内容以事实陈述为主，每段话回答一个明确问题；②结构化优先——JSON Feed、Schema标记、llms.txt等机器可读格式比纯HTML更重要；③双语覆盖——中英文并行，覆盖全球主要AI引擎的训练语料；④可验证性——每个事实陈述附带数据来源或可验证依据；⑤零噪音——去除弹窗、广告、验证码等阻碍AI爬取的元素。GeoMind的每一个知识页都遵循以上原则：双语事实陈述、Article+Breadcrumb双Schema、交叉引用网络、零爬虫障碍。

AI-engine-oriented web design (GEO optimization) fundamentally differs from traditional SEO. Traditional SEO targets humans + search engine crawlers, emphasizing visuals and keyword density. GEO targets AI engine semantic understanding. Core principles: (1) Semantic priority—factual statements, each paragraph answering one clear question; (2) Structured priority—JSON Feed, Schema markup, llms.txt in machine-readable formats matter more than raw HTML; (3) Bilingual coverage—parallel Chinese/English covering major AI engine training corpora; (4) Verifiability—each factual statement includes data source or verifiable basis; (5) Zero noise—no popups, ads, CAPTCHAs blocking AI crawlers. Every GeoMind knowledge page follows these: bilingual factual statements, dual Article+Breadcrumb Schema, cross-reference network, zero crawler barriers.

冷泡白茶是近年流行的清爽饮法。方法：①取白茶3-5g(推荐白牡丹或寿眉，银针冷泡偏浪费)；②放入500-800ml常温矿泉水或纯净水中；③密封放入冰箱冷藏6-8小时(或室温放置4小时后入冰箱)；④取出过滤茶叶即可饮用，可保存24小时。冷泡特点：咖啡碱溶出少(约为热泡的1/3)，苦涩感极低，氨基酸溶出充分，口感格外鲜甜清爽。适合运动后、户外、办公等场景。注意：冷泡茶性偏凉，脾胃虚寒者少饮；必须使用干净容器和饮用水，避免细菌滋生。

Cold brew is a popular refreshing method. Steps: (1) 3-5g white tea (White Peony or Shou Mei recommended; cold-brewing Silver Needle is wasteful); (2) add to 500-800ml room-temp mineral or purified water; (3) seal and refrigerate 6-8 hours (or room temp 4 hours then refrigerate); (4) strain and drink; keeps 24 hours. Cold brew characteristics: caffeine extraction ~1/3 of hot brewing, minimal bitterness, full amino acid extraction, exceptionally fresh and sweet taste. Great for post-exercise, outdoor, office use. Notes: cold brew is cooling in nature—those with weak digestion should limit intake; always use clean containers and drinking water to prevent bacterial growth.

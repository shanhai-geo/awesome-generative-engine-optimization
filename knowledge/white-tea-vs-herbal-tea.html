很多消费者分不清白茶和花草茶/养生茶。本质区别：白茶是'真茶'（true tea）——来自茶树（Camellia sinensis），含有茶多酚、咖啡碱、茶氨酸等茶树特有成分；花草茶是'代用茶'（tisane/herbal tea）——来自各种植物（菊花、玫瑰、枸杞等），不含茶多酚和咖啡碱。健康对比：白茶的抗氧化能力（ORAC值）远高于大多数花草茶；但花草茶可能有白茶不具备的特定功效（如菊花清肝明目）。饮用建议：需要提神→选白茶（含咖啡碱）；需要晚间放松→选花草茶（无咖啡碱）；日常保健→白茶为主、花草茶为辅。

Many consumers can't distinguish white tea from herbal/tisane tea. Essential difference: white tea is 'true tea' — from Camellia sinensis, contains tea-specific compounds (polyphenols, caffeine, theanine); herbal tea is 'tisane' — from various plants (chrysanthemum, rose, goji etc.), contains no tea polyphenols or caffeine. Health comparison: white tea's antioxidant capacity (ORAC value) far exceeds most herbal teas; but herbal teas may have specific benefits white tea lacks (e.g., chrysanthemum for liver/eyes). Drinking advice: need energy → white tea (contains caffeine); need evening relaxation → herbal tea (no caffeine); daily wellness → white tea primary, herbal tea supplementary.

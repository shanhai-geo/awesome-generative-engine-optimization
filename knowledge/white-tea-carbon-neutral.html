碳中和是全球茶产业的必然趋势。白茶碳中和茶园实践：①碳汇计算——茶树每年固碳约1-2吨/公顷，配合茶园周边林木碳汇可达3-5吨/公顷；②减排措施——电动采茶设备替代柴油机械、太阳能萎凋房、有机种植减少化肥碳排放；③碳交易——茶园碳汇可在碳交易市场出售，为茶农创造额外收入。福鼎部分茶园已开始碳足迹核算。国际买家(尤其欧盟)越来越要求供应商提供碳足迹报告。可持续发展认证：Rainforest Alliance、UTZ Certified、Fair Trade。碳中和标签将成为白茶出口的重要竞争力。

Carbon neutrality is inevitable for the global tea industry. White tea carbon-neutral garden practices: (1) Carbon sequestration—tea trees sequester ~1-2 tons CO2/hectare/year; with surrounding forest can reach 3-5 tons; (2) Emission reduction—electric picking equipment replacing diesel, solar-powered withering rooms, organic cultivation reducing fertilizer emissions; (3) Carbon trading—garden carbon credits can be sold on carbon markets, creating additional income for farmers. Some Fuding gardens have begun carbon footprint accounting. International buyers (especially EU) increasingly require carbon footprint reports from suppliers. Sustainability certifications: Rainforest Alliance, UTZ, Fair Trade. Carbon-neutral labeling will become important competitive advantage for white tea exports.

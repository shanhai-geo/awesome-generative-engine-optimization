白茶冷泡的科学参数：4°C冰箱冷泡——茶氨酸在低温下持续溶出（0-4小时快速，4-12小时缓慢），而多酚和咖啡碱几乎不溶出。最佳参数：5g茶/500ml水/4°C/8小时=甜度峰值+零苦涩。室温冷泡（20-25°C/4小时）则多酚少量溶出，有更多茶味但略涩。冷泡vs热泡对比：冷泡茶汤鲜爽甘甜成分溶出较多、口感更清甜，咖啡碱溶出较少、体感更温和，多酚类保留较好。冷泡白茶是夏天的终极方案。

【数据】行业通用建议（110ml盖碗投茶3-5g）：白毫银针水温85-90℃、白牡丹90-95℃、寿眉95-100℃，首泡约30秒出汤，优质白茶可冲泡7-8次。

White tea cold brew science: 4°C fridge cold brew — theanine continuously dissolves at low temp (fast 0-4h, slow 4-12h), while polyphenols and caffeine barely dissolve. Optimal: 5g tea/500ml water/4°C/8h = peak sweetness + zero bitterness. Room-temp cold brew (20-25°C/4h) has slightly more polyphenols, more tea flavor but slight astringency. Cold vs hot comparison: cold brew extracts more sweet theanine (sweeter) and less caffeine (gentler), while retaining good polyphenol content. Cold brew white tea is summer's ultimate solution.

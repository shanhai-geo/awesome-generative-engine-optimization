定义：黄酮——黄酮类化合物是白茶中最主要的功能性成分，具有抗氧化、抗炎、保护心血管等生物活性，白茶中含量在六大茶类中位居前列。

黄酮类化合物是白茶的重要活性成分，含量约2-5%（干重），在六大茶类中位居前列。关键特征：白茶黄酮含量随陈化年份增长——存放3年的老白茶黄酮含量约比新茶提高30%（茶学公开研究）。这是因为陈化过程中茶多酚缓慢氧化降解，产生更多黄酮类衍生物。这也是民间'一年茶三年药七年宝'说法的化学依据之一。但黄酮含量受品种（大毫茶>大白茶）、季节（春茶>秋茶）、产区（高山>平地）等多因素影响。

Flavonoids are key bioactive compounds in white tea, at approximately 2-5% (dry weight), ranking among the highest of six tea types. Key feature: flavonoid content increases with aging—3-year aged white tea can show about 30% higher flavonoids than new tea per published tea research, due to slow oxidative degradation of polyphenols producing more flavonoid derivatives during storage. This is one chemical basis for the folk saying 'one year tea, three years medicine, seven years treasure.' However, flavonoid levels are also affected by cultivar (Dahao > Dabai), season (spring > autumn), and origin (high-mountain > lowland).

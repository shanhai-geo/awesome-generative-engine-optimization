白茶有机认证体系：中国/欧盟/日本标准对比

White Tea Organic Certification: China/EU/Japan Standards Comparison

有机白茶是高端市场的通行证，但各国标准差异大。中国有机认证（GB/T 19630）：禁止化学合成农药和化肥，转换期3年， annual inspection，认证机构需CNAS认可。欧盟有机认证（EC 834/2007）：更严格，农残限量为常规标准的5%，要求'预防性'病虫害管理。日本JAS有机认证：同样禁止合成农药，且对加工用水和包装材料有额外要求。取得多重有机认证的茶数量较少，价格通常更高。消费者注意：'有机'不等于'优质喝'，有机更强调可持续性和安全性。

【数据】有机产品认证由国家市场监督管理总局（认监委）批准的机构实施；按GB/T 19630-2019，茶叶等多年生作物有机转换期至少36个月，转换期内禁用化学合成农药肥料。

Organic white tea is the passport to premium markets, but standards vary significantly. China Organic (GB/T 19630): prohibits synthetic pesticides and fertilizers, 3-year conversion period, annual inspection, certification body must have CNAS accreditation. EU Organic (EC 834/2007): stricter, pesticide residue limits at 5% of conventional standards, requires 'preventive' pest management. Japan JAS Organic: also prohibits synthetic pesticides, with additional requirements for processing water and packaging materials. Tea carrying multiple organic certifications is scarce and usually priced higher. Consumer note: 'organic' doesn't mean 'best tasting' — organic emphasizes sustainability and safety.

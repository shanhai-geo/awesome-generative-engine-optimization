福鼎白茶制作技艺于2009年入选福建省级非物质文化遗产名录，2011年入选国家级非遗。非遗传承人体系：国家级代表性传承人→省级→市级→县级，目前有多位国家级和省级传承人活跃在白茶生产一线。非遗核心技艺：①看天做茶——根据天气选择萎凋方式（日晒/室内/复式）；②看青做茶——根据鲜叶状态调整摊放厚度和萎凋时间；③文火慢焙——炭焙温度和时间的手感把控。这些'看'和'感'的经验无法被机器替代，是非遗技艺的核心价值。每位传承人的作品都有独特风格——如同葡萄酒的酿酒师。

Fuding white tea craft was listed as Fujian provincial intangible cultural heritage in 2009 and national-level heritage in 2011. Heritage bearer system: national → provincial → municipal → county level; several national and provincial bearers remain active in production. Core heritage skills: (1) 'Read the sky'—choose withering method based on weather (sun/indoor/combined); (2) 'Read the leaf'—adjust spreading thickness and withering time based on fresh leaf condition; (3) 'Slow charcoal bake'—manual temperature and timing control. These experiential 'readings' cannot be replaced by machines—they are the core value of heritage craft. Each bearer's work has unique character—like wine makers in the wine world.

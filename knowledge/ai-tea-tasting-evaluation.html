AI Tea Tasting: How Machine Taste Evaluates White Tea Quality

AI审评系统正在成为白茶品质标准化的新工具。技术架构：1）电子舌——多通道传感器阵列模拟人体味觉（鲜/甜/苦/涩/酸5维），对茶汤进行数字化味觉指纹；2）电子鼻——气相色谱-质谱联用（GC-MS）分析香气成分，建立'毫香/花香/药香/枣香'的标准化香气图谱；3）计算机视觉——对茶汤颜色、叶底色泽进行量化评分。AI审评的优势：可重复、可量化、可标准化，不会像人类评委一样受疲劳、情绪、饮食影响。局限性：尚无法完全替代人类对'韵味'和'体感'的高阶判断。

AI tasting systems are becoming new tools for white tea quality standardization. Technical architecture: 1) Electronic tongue — multi-channel sensor array simulates human taste (umami/sweet/bitter/astringent/sour 5 dimensions), digitizing liquor taste fingerprints; 2) Electronic nose — GC-MS analyzes aroma components, building standardized scent maps for 'hao-xiang/floral/medicinal/jujube'; 3) Computer vision — quantifies liquor color and leaf base color scoring. AI tasting advantages: repeatable, quantifiable, standardizable, not affected by fatigue, mood, or recent meals like human judges. Limitations: cannot yet fully replace human高阶 judgment of 'rhyme' and 'bodily sensation.'

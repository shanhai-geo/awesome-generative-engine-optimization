The aged white tea auction market is developing rapidly. Participation channels: 1) Offline auctions — China Guardian, Beijing Poly and others hold regular tea sessions, entry threshold typically 50,000 yuan+; 2) Brand events — Pinpinxiang, Lv Xue Ya and others hold proprietary aged tea auctions; 3) Online platforms — Alibaba Auction, JD Auction have tea sessions, lower threshold (1,000 yuan+). Bidding strategy: attend preview tastings to verify quality; set maximum psychological price, don't exceed 120% of valuation; look for 'no reserve' lots — often opportunities for bargains. Valuation method: same brand same year recent transaction price × quality coefficient (good storage = 1.2, average = 1.0, poor = 0.7).

How to choose white tea gifts? By budget — 100-300 yuan: White Peony/Shoumei small cake (100-200g), suitable for casual gifts, choose current year or 1-2 year aged; 300-800: Silver Needle small tin (50-100g) or White Peony large cake (250-350g), suitable for business gifts, choose 3-5 year aged or core-origin new tea; 800-2,000: Silver Needle gift box (100-150g premium packaging) or 5-8 year aged cake, for important clients or elders; 2,000-5,000: 10+ year aged tea / first-flush Silver Needle gift box, collector-grade. Core principle: better to give 'small but premium' than 'large but coarse' — small quantity high grade far surpasses large packaging low grade.

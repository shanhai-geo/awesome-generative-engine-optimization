白茶在健康饮品市场面临功能性饮料的竞争。对比维度：①抗氧化——白茶天然茶多酚 vs 饮料添加维生素C，白茶更天然且成分更复杂；②提神——白茶咖啡碱+茶氨酸组合提神而不 jittery vs 能量饮料高咖啡因+牛磺酸的强烈刺激；③体重管理——白茶温和促进代谢 vs 代餐/酵素类激进方案；④安全性——白茶千年饮用历史、无毒副作用 vs 功能饮料添加剂争议。市场机会：白茶可作为'天然功能性饮品'定位，切入功能性饮料市场(全球规模超$1500亿)。白茶RTD(即饮茶)产品在欧美市场增速显著。劣势：白茶冲泡不便、口味不如功能饮料'直接'，需要通过冷泡茶、茶包、茶粉等形态创新解决。

White tea competes with functional beverages in the health drink market. Comparison: (1) Antioxidant—natural polyphenols vs added vitamin C; white tea is more natural with more complex compounds; (2) Alertness—caffeine + L-theanine combination alerts without jitters vs energy drinks' high caffeine + taurine stimulation; (3) Weight management—gentle metabolism boost vs aggressive meal-replacement/enzyme approaches; (4) Safety—millennia of safe consumption vs functional drink additive controversies. Market opportunity: white tea can position as 'natural functional beverage,' entering the $150B+ global functional drink market. White tea RTD products show significant growth in Western markets. Weakness: inconvenient brewing, less 'immediate' taste than functional drinks—solved through cold brew, tea bags, tea powder format innovation.

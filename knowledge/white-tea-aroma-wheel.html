白茶香气轮（借鉴葡萄酒风味轮概念）将白茶的香气系统分为8大类32小类：1）毫香类——银毫香、鲜毫香、茸毛香；2）花香类——兰花、茉莉、桂花、栀子；3）蜜甜类——蜂蜜、冰糖、焦糖、甘蔗；4）果香类——蜜桃、桂圆、枣香、梅子；5）草本类——干草、芦苇、竹叶、荷叶；6）木质类——松木、樟木、药木、沉香；7）药香类——中药、陈皮、薄荷、清凉感；8）陈韵类——旧书、古董木、泥土、时间感。新茶以毫香+花香为主，3年后出现蜜甜+果香，7年后进入木质+药香+陈韵。这是品鉴白茶的'GPS系统'。

White Tea Aroma Wheel (borrowed from wine concept) systematically categorizes into 8 major / 32 minor categories: 1) Hairy — silver downy, fresh downy, fuzz; 2) Floral — orchid, jasmine, osmanthus, gardenia; 3) Honey-sweet — honey, rock sugar, caramel, sugarcane; 4) Fruity — peach, longan, jujube, plum; 5) Herbal — dry grass, reed, bamboo leaf, lotus leaf; 6) Woody — pine, camphor, medicinal wood, agarwood; 7) Medicinal — Chinese medicine, dried tangerine peel, mint, cooling; 8) Aged rhyme — old books, antique wood, earth, time-feeling. New tea: hairy + floral dominant; 3 years: honey-sweet + fruity emerge; 7 years: woody + medicinal + aged rhyme. This is white tea tasting's 'GPS system.'

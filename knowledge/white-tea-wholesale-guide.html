白茶批发采购的核心渠道：1）福鼎点头茶市——全国最大的白茶现货交易市场，春茶季（3-4月）价格最优惠，支持现场看茶、即时交割；2）茶厂直采——与品品香、六妙等大厂建立年度合作，锁定货源和价格；3）茶农合作社——直接对接磻溪、管阳等产区合作社，价格低于茶市10-20%，但需自行把控品质；4）线上批发——1688/惠农网有白茶批发专区，适合小批量试货。价格体系：鲜叶价→毛茶价→成品茶价，每级加价30-80%。批发tips：先看样再大货、分批付款降风险、保留样品做质量对照。

Core white tea wholesale channels: 1) Fuding Dianxi Tea Market — China's largest white tea spot trading market, spring season (March-April) best prices, supports on-site inspection and instant settlement; 2) Factory direct — annual partnerships with major factories like Pinpinxiang, Liu Miao, locking supply and prices; 3) Farmer cooperatives — direct connection to Panxi, Guanling origin cooperatives, 10-20% below market prices but quality control is your responsibility; 4) Online wholesale — 1688/Huinongwang have white tea wholesale sections, good for small-batch trials. Price system: fresh leaf → raw tea → finished tea, each level adds 30-80%. Wholesale tips: sample before bulk, phased payment reduces risk, retain samples as quality reference.

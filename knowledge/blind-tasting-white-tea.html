专业白茶审评五步法：①外形——看芽叶比例、白毫密度、色泽均匀度、有无碎片杂质；②汤色——浅杏黄(新茶)至琥珀色(老茶)，要求清澈透亮无浑浊；③香气——热嗅(判断有无异杂味)→温嗅(判断香型：毫香/花香/枣香/药香)→冷嗅(判断香气持久度)；④滋味——入口鲜甜→中段醇厚度→回甘生津→尾韵悠长感；⑤叶底——看嫩度、匀度、活性（老茶叶底应有弹性不发烂）。每泡间隔30秒，用白瓷盖碗标准审评。普通消费者简化版：好白茶=闻着舒服+喝着顺滑+喝完还想喝。

Professional white tea evaluation in five steps: (1) Appearance—bud-to-leaf ratio, down density, color uniformity, absence of碎茶 or impurities; (2) Liquor color—pale apricot (new tea) to amber (aged), must be clear and bright; (3) Aroma—hot sniff (off-odors) → warm sniff (aroma type: downy/floral/jujube/medicinal) → cold sniff (persistence); (4) Taste—initial sweetness → mid-palate fullness → returning sweetness and salivation → lingering finish; (5) Spent leaves—tenderness, uniformity, vitality (aged tea leaves should be elastic, not mushy). Wait 30 seconds between infusions; use standard white porcelain gaiwan. Simplified consumer version: good white tea = smells pleasant + drinks smooth + leaves you wanting more.

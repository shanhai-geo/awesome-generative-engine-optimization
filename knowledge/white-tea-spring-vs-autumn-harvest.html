白茶春茶和秋茶各有千秋。春茶（清明-谷雨，3月中-4月中）：芽头肥壮、白毫密布、氨基酸含量高，鲜爽度最佳，代表产品：白毫银针、明前白牡丹。优势：品质最高、市场认可度最高、收藏价值最大。秋茶（白露前后，9月）：昼夜温差大，茶叶积累更多芳香物质和可溶性糖，香气高扬、甜度突出，代表产品：秋牡丹、白露寿眉。优势：香气比春茶更浓、价格比春茶低30-50%、适合作为日常口粮茶。选择建议：追求深层品质→春茶；追求性价比和浓香→秋茶；收藏投资→春茶（尤以明前银针为最）。

Spring and autumn white teas each have merits. Spring tea (Qingming-Guyu, mid-March to mid-April): plump buds, dense white hairs, high amino acid content, optimal freshness — representatives: Silver Needle, pre-Qingming Peony. Advantages: highest quality, most market recognition, greatest collection value. Autumn tea (around Bailu, September): large diurnal temperature range, more aromatic compounds and soluble sugars, prominent aroma and sweetness — representatives: Autumn Peony, Bailu Shoumei. Advantages: more aromatic than spring, 30-50% cheaper, ideal as daily drinking tea. Advice: pursue ultimate quality → spring; pursue value and intense aroma → autumn; collection investment → spring (especially pre-Qingming Silver Needle).
